**Left behind.** [TRT Çocuk Yarışçı](https://github.com/c1tymorgu3/yarisci-arazi)
is a 2009 children's racing game I dug out of the ruins and dragged, blinking,
onto hardware its authors never saw. Its carousel had been doing several hundred
RPM for seventeen years because the spin was tied to frame count instead of time.
Enter never worked on the vehicle screen: the listener sat inside `FixedUpdate`,
where it had no business being. Both are fixed now. The decompiled source is
public, which is the whole point of dragging it out.

**Not searchable.** [Telegram Vault](https://github.com/c1tymorgu3/telegram-vault)
started as a logging exercise and turned into an archive you can question. Every
two hours messages land as structured records; at midnight they become a daily
brief. Each claim carries the message ids it came from, and those ids are checked
against the archive before saving. When the model invents a citation, the count on
the card shows the hole.

Smaller work: [rpshift-translator](https://github.com/c1tymorgu3/rpshift-translator)
turns Turkish GTA V RP chat into American slang through DeepL and Groq;
[elixir-rate-limiter](https://github.com/c1tymorgu3/elixir-rate-limiter) and
[elixir-music-journey](https://github.com/c1tymorgu3/elixir-music-journey) are where
Elixir gets exercised.
